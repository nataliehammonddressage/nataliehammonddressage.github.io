About

Hi, I'm Natalie Hammond

Natalie Hammond (Ottiger) started riding horses at an early age, which has allowed her to compete and train internationally.

Natalie has successfully competed at FEI levels, both in Germany and Australia. After studying and training in Germany for three years, she completed her Bereiter training with the Equestrian Association e.V. in Warendorf, Germany.

Her talent and love of horses have allowed her to reach levels of competition few riders get to, and to help her students reach beyond their own riding goals. More recently she qualified for and competed at the US Dressage Finals in 2022 and 2023.

Natalie now rides and trains out of Paradiso Farm in Wentzville, Missouri. She lives in Wildwood, Missouri with her husband Chris and their kids.
