Some news from our corner of the dressage world: Natalie is now riding and training out of [Paradiso Farm](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61592222600694) in Wentzville, Missouri.

These days Natalie is spending more time in the saddle on her own horses and a little less time teaching, so this website will be a lot more about riding, showing, and photos from the barn. Keep an eye on the [blog](/blog/) and the [photo albums](/photos/) for updates.

She still has a limited number of openings for [dressage lessons and training](/training/). If you're interested, [get in touch](/contact/) and she'll let you know what's available.
