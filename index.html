About Natalie

A life in the saddle

Natalie has ridden since childhood and has competed at FEI levels in Germany and Australia. After three years of study in Germany she completed her Bereiter training with the Equestrian Association e.V. in Warendorf, and she has qualified for and competed at the US Dressage Finals.

She lives in Wildwood, Missouri with her husband Chris and their kids.

Lessons & training at Paradiso Farm
