Training

Dressage Training & Lessons in St. Louis

Lessons, training rides and show support with Natalie Hammond at Paradiso Farm in Wentzville, Missouri.

Finding a dressage trainer who will work with you and your horse is an important part of becoming a rider who earns high marks in the show ring. Natalie works with riders and horses of all levels to raise the bar on their performance at home or in competition.

Natalie competed at FEI levels in Germany and Australia, completed her Bereiter training in Warendorf, Germany, and has qualified for and competed at the US Dressage Finals. Read more in her bio and CV.

Training options

Natalie keeps a limited schedule, with availability for:

  • Private dressage lessons
  • Full and partial training for your horse
  • Show preparation and show-day assistance
  • Clinics

Frequently asked questions

Where does Natalie teach and train?
Natalie rides and trains out of Paradiso Farm in Wentzville, Missouri, convenient to St. Louis, Wildwood, O'Fallon, Chesterfield and the rest of St. Charles County.
Who does Natalie work with?
Riders and horses at every level, from those just starting dressage to competitors working toward regional championships and the US Dressage Finals.
Is Natalie taking new students?
Natalie keeps a limited schedule these days. Openings for lessons and training come up from time to time, so please get in touch to ask about current availability and pricing.

Ask about availability

Please contact Natalie for current availability and pricing, and with any questions about dressage training in the St. Louis area. Looking for a place for your horse? See dressage boarding.
